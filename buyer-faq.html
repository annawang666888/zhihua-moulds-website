Buyer FAQ for Precast Concrete Moulds

Practical answers for overseas buyers before requesting drainage, kerbstone, paver, slope protection, utility or custom precast concrete moulds.

Customization & OEM

Can you make moulds from drawings?

Yes. Send 2D drawings, 3D files, section drawings or local-standard dimensions. We review the mould structure, demoulding direction and material options before quotation.

Can you quote from sample photos?

Yes. Clear front, side and top photos with a ruler or known-size reference can support an initial review. Drawings are still preferred for final production.

Can logos, letters or project markings be added?

Often yes, if the pattern does not block demoulding. Send the artwork or marking requirement with the finished concrete dimensions.

Quotation Information

  • Finished concrete product name and application
  • Length, width, height, thickness and profile drawing
  • Quantity and whether mixed mould types are needed
  • Destination country and preferred shipping method
  • Material preference if known: PP, ABS or PE
  • Photos of similar products or current moulds

Samples, MOQ & Lead Time

Is there a fixed MOQ?

MOQ depends on mould size, existing stock, customization level and packing method. Small trial orders can be discussed for standard items.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on mould size, structure, material, quantity and factory schedule. Standard items are usually faster; OEM moulds need design and production review.

Are samples free?

Sample policy is confirmed case by case. For export orders, sample cost and freight should be discussed before production or shipment.

Packing, Shipping & Payment

Can you mix different moulds in one shipment?

Yes. Mixed-SKU packing is common for distributors, precast factories and contractors who need drainage, kerbstone, paver and utility moulds together.

Which documents can be supported?

Commercial invoice, packing list, loading photos and other normal export documents can be coordinated according to the order.

Which ports are suitable?

Shipment can normally be arranged from major China ports such as Shanghai or Ningbo depending on order routing and freight plan.

Want a faster reply?

Send product dimensions, photos/drawings, quantity and destination country in your first message. We can check catalogue options or custom mould feasibility.
