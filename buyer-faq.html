Buyer FAQ

Precast Concrete Mould Buyer FAQ

Answers for overseas buyers preparing quotations for drainage, kerbstone, paver, manhole, cable trench, slope protection and custom precast concrete moulds.

Quotation Information

What should I send for a fast quote?

Send the finished concrete product name, length, width, height, thickness or diameter, photos or drawings, quantity, destination country and application. If the product follows a local road or utility standard, include that standard drawing.

Can you quote from only photos?

Clear photos can support an initial review, especially if they include a ruler or known-size reference. For final production, drawings or confirmed dimensions are strongly preferred.

Why do you ask for destination country?

Destination helps discuss packing, loading, shipping route, local terminology and sometimes product standard. It also helps us suggest related moulds that fit your market.

Customization & OEM

Can you make moulds from drawings?

Yes. Send 2D CAD, PDF drawing, 3D model or section drawing. We review demoulding direction, wall thickness, corners, surface pattern, logo/marking feasibility and material options.

Can logos or project markings be added?

Often yes, if the pattern does not block demoulding or weaken the mould. Send artwork, text height and position requirements with the finished concrete dimensions.

Can you help if I only know the application?

Yes. Tell us whether it is for road drainage, sidewalks, municipal wells, cable utility corridors, slope protection, railway, power or solar projects. We can point you to relevant catalogue categories.

MOQ, Samples & Lead Time

Is there a fixed MOQ?

MOQ depends on mould size, existing stock, customization level and packing method. Small trial orders can be discussed for standard items; custom profiles need separate review.

How long does production take?

Lead time depends on product structure, material, quantity and factory schedule. Standard catalogue moulds are usually faster; OEM moulds need drawing confirmation and production review.

Are samples available?

Sample policy is confirmed case by case. For export orders, sample cost, mould type and freight should be agreed before production or shipment.

Packing, Shipping & Documents

Can different moulds be mixed in one shipment?

Yes. Mixed-SKU packing is common for distributors, precast factories and contractors who need drainage, kerbstone, paver, utility and custom moulds together.

Which export documents can be supported?

Commercial invoice, packing list, loading photos and other normal export documents can be coordinated according to the order. Special document needs should be raised before quotation.

Can I request packing or loading photos?

Yes, packing and loading photos can be discussed, especially for mixed-SKU or container orders where buyers need internal records.

Product-Specific Questions

If you need...Send these details first
Drainage channel or U-drain mouldsInner channel width, depth, length, cover slab details, road/load application and quantity.
Kerbstone or curbstone mouldsProfile drawing, length, height, top/bottom width, slope, radius/corner needs and local standard.
Paver block mouldsPattern, dimensions, thickness, surface finish, logo/marking and expected production volume.
Manhole cover or well brick mouldsShape, diameter or outer size, thickness, well-ring diameter, brick size and installation purpose.
Cable trench or utility mouldsU-channel size, cover size, cable/power/telecom use, straight or turning pieces and project drawings.

Want the fastest reply?

Send product dimensions, photos/drawings, quantity and destination country in your first message. We can check catalogue options or custom mould feasibility.
