Cable Trench Moulds Buying Guide: Cable Duct, Trough and Utility Channel Moulds

How to choose cable trench moulds for power, telecom, railway and municipal utility corridor projects, including U-shaped trench bodies, covers and OEM quote details.

Published June 14, 2026 · Zhihua Moulds

Where cable trench moulds are used

Power cable trenches, telecom ducts, railway cable channels, industrial parks, substations, municipal utility corridors and drainage-linked service trenches are the main application scenarios. Buyers usually need stable finished concrete dimensions, clean demoulding and repeatable production for project quantities.

Main mould types to compare

U-shaped cable trench moulds, utility trench covers, small cable duct moulds, deep trench moulds and custom cable trough profiles can be supplied as catalogue-style products or reviewed as custom moulds from project drawings. The correct choice depends on the finished concrete part, installation method and required surface details.

Key specification details

  • Finished cable trench length × width × height and wall thickness
  • Open U-channel, covered trench, split cover or special utility duct type
  • Cable load/application: power, telecom, railway, substation, industrial park or municipal corridor
  • Required lifting holes, ribs, drainage holes, cover groove or marking details

Material and customization notes

For infrastructure components, the mould should match the finished concrete geometry rather than only the product name. Send drawings, section dimensions, sample photos and project usage details so Zhihua can check mould feasibility, packing and quotation accurately.

How to request a quote

Prepare the finished product size, order quantity, destination country and any local-standard or marking requirements. For OEM work, include a drawing or clear photo from several angles.
