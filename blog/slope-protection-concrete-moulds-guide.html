Frequently asked questions

What does a wall thickness marked 6–5 cm on a hollow revetment block mean?

It is a tapered wall: 6 cm at the wide face and 5 cm at the narrow face. The taper lets the cast block release cleanly from the mould and is standard practice on hollow revetment blocks — it is a draft angle, not a tolerance or a defect.

How do I choose the right hexagonal revetment block size?

Side length 14.5–18 cm suits canal linings and low-flow banks, 20 cm is the most widely used size for river banks and embankments, and 22–25 cm is specified for high-flow channels and main river works. Block height then follows the design concrete volume. Note the hexagon is quoted by side length, not by the diagonal.

Do you supply half-hexagon blocks for finishing the mat edge?

Yes. Hollow half-hexagon and semi-hollow moulds in 15×20, 17×8, 20×6, 20×8, 20×12, 20×15, 20×40×12 and 25×20 cm let the revetment mat finish at the edge without cutting blocks.

What is the benefit of hollow ecological slope blocks?

The hollow area supports vegetation or drainage while the concrete frame protects the slope from erosion and interlocks with the neighbouring units. Interlocking and stepped (I-shaped and half-I) blocks add shear keying between units for embankments and river banks.

What information do you need for a slope block quote?

Send the finished block length, width, thickness and opening size, the block type (hexagonal, hollow, interlocking, grass-planting, river-bank or canal type), the application, any logo or pattern requirement, quantity and destination country. Mixed-size packing can be arranged based on quantity and mould size.
