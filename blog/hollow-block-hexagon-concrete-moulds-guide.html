Hollow Block & Hexagon Concrete Moulds Guide: Paving and Revetment

A practical guide to hollow concrete block moulds, hollow hexagon slope protection moulds and flat hexagon paving slab moulds for overseas projects.

One category, several overseas use cases

Hollow block and hexagon moulds connect paver/block demand with infrastructure slope-protection demand. They can be used for hardscape paving, ecological revetment, river bank protection, drainage channel sides and landscape projects.

Main product types

  • Hollow concrete block moulds for block and paving production
  • Multi-hole concrete block moulds for lightweight concrete components
  • Hollow hexagon block moulds for slope protection and erosion control
  • Flat hexagon paving slab moulds for walkways and garden surfaces

Quote details to prepare

Buyers should provide outside dimensions, hole size, wall thickness, target application, quantity and destination country. If the concrete product needs special surface texture, logo or lettering, include the drawing before quotation.

Related product lines

This category pairs well with paver block moulds, slope protection moulds, kerbstone moulds and custom precast concrete moulds.
