Photovoltaic Pier Moulds Guide: Solar Support and PV Foundation Bases

How to select photovoltaic pier moulds for solar support bases, ground-mounted PV foundations, cable grooves and custom renewable-energy project components.

Published June 14, 2026 · Zhihua Moulds

Where photovoltaic pier moulds are used

Ground-mounted solar farms, photovoltaic support foundations, distributed PV bases, cable-supported solar pier systems and renewable-energy infrastructure projects are the main application scenarios. Buyers usually need stable finished concrete dimensions, clean demoulding and repeatable production for project quantities.

Main mould types to compare

Straight solar pier moulds, grooved PV base moulds, double-slot photovoltaic pier moulds, U-type support pier moulds and custom foundation components can be supplied as catalogue-style products or reviewed as custom moulds from project drawings. The correct choice depends on the finished concrete part, installation method and required surface details.

Key specification details

  • Finished pier length × width × height and required groove/slot detail
  • Support type: straight base, double-slot, U-type, cable-supported or custom foundation
  • Installation environment: ground-mounted solar farm, rooftop base, distributed PV or utility-scale project
  • Embedded parts, lifting hole, reinforcement or cable channel requirements

Material and customization notes

For infrastructure components, the mould should match the finished concrete geometry rather than only the product name. Send drawings, section dimensions, sample photos and project usage details so Zhihua can check mould feasibility, packing and quotation accurately.

How to request a quote

Prepare the finished product size, order quantity, destination country and any local-standard or marking requirements. For OEM work, include a drawing or clear photo from several angles.
