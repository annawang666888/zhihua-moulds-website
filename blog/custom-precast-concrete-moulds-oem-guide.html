Custom Precast Concrete Moulds OEM Guide: Drawings, Samples, ABS, PP and PE Materials

What customers should prepare for custom precast concrete moulds, including drawings, sample photos, dimensions, applications, ABS/PP/PE materials, logos and patterns.

Published June 15, 2026 · Zhihua Moulds

Where these moulds are used

Drainage channels, kerbstones, pavers, manhole covers, cable trenches, power engineering parts, photovoltaic piers, slope protection blocks, railway components and other custom precast products

Main product scope

Custom ABS moulds, PP moulds, PE moulds, logo moulds, letter/pattern moulds, catalogue-size modification and new project component moulds

Customization details to confirm

  • Finished concrete product dimensions and section drawing
  • Clear sample photos from multiple angles if no drawing is available
  • Application and installation environment
  • Estimated quantity and target country
  • Preferred material if known: ABS, PP or PE
  • Logo, letters, surface pattern, marking or local-standard details

OEM and material notes

Zhihua can review standard catalogue options or custom mould feasibility from drawings, dimensions and sample photos. For custom plastic moulds, ABS, PP and PE are the main material options. Logo, letters and patterns can be customized when the mould structure allows.

Requesting a quotation

Prepare the finished product size, application, order quantity, target country, material preference and any drawing or sample photo. This reduces back-and-forth and helps the factory review feasibility faster.
