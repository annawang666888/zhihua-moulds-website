Road Shoulder Stone & Bridge Cap Mould Buyer Guide

Highway shoulder stones, sloped/perforated edge stones, retaining blocks and bridge cap coping — match the mould to the finished cross-section, drainage and load.

Four mould groups on this page

  • Road shoulder stones: flat edge stones 49.5–60cm long, 8–26cm thick, for the pavement-to-shoulder edge.
  • Perforated / sloped / beveled: stones with a pitched top, chamfered face, or moulded drain hole (1-hole, half-round Ø19) for edge drainage.
  • Cover slabs, retaining & notched pieces: shoulder cover slabs (13.8cm), tall retaining blocks (36.7cm) and left/right notched end units.
  • Bridge caps & L-caps: thick coping caps (50–60cm wide, 20–33.5cm high) for piers/abutments, plus L-shaped corner caps.

Three things buyers get wrong

  • Confusing shoulder stones with bridge caps. They share a photo style but are different units — thin drainage edge stone vs thick structural coping cap. Order from the cross-section drawing, not the name alone.
  • Treating left/right notched pieces as interchangeable. On a sloped stone the notch hand is fixed; count right-notch and left-notch separately.
  • Planning to drill holes or cut bevels after casting. Holes and slopes are moulded in; drilling/chiselling cured concrete chips the face and weakens the edge.

Road Shoulder Stone & Bridge Cap Mould FAQ

What is the difference between a road shoulder stone and a bridge cap (taimao)?

Road shoulder stones (路肩石) are the edge blocks along a highway shoulder — typically 49.5–75cm long, 8–26cm thick, flat or sloped/beveled on top, sometimes with a drain hole or an end notch. They separate the pavement from the shoulder and carry edge drainage. Bridge caps / taimao (台帽) are the thicker coping blocks (30–41cm wide, 20–33.5cm high) that sit on top of bridge piers, abutments or retaining-wall posts as a load-distribution cap; L-shaped caps (L型台帽) wrap a corner. They are heavier structural units, not drainage edge stones.

What do sloped, beveled and flat shoulder stones mean?

Flat shoulder stones (平石) have a level top. Sloped (坡型) stones have a pitched top that sheds water off the pavement; beveled (斜面) stones have a chamfered face (e.g. a 14cm bevel on the 50×50×15). The slope/bevel is cast into the mould and is not cut afterwards, so quote the finished cross-section drawing. Perforated versions (1-hole, half-round Ø19 hole, perforated sloped) drain through the stone.

How do the notched shoulder stone pieces fit?

Notched pieces (缺口1 right notch, 缺口2 left notch on the 55×50×10.3 stone) are end units with a side opening that lets water or an adjacent block interlock at a node. Left and right are separate moulds — order them by hand and count, since a right-notch piece cannot be flipped to make a left-notch run when the top is sloped.

Are the drain holes drilled on site?

No. The 1-hole, half-round-hole (Ø19) and perforated sloped shoulder stones are moulded with the hole as part of the concrete shape. Give the hole size and position; each layout is a separate mould. Do not drill cured stones — it chips the sloped face and weakens the edge.

What are the retaining block and shoulder cover slab for?

The retaining block (挡土块, 50×35×36.7 with a 20cm sloped face) is a tall shoulder unit that holds back fill at the pavement edge. The shoulder cover slab (路肩盖板, 50×49×13.8) caps a shoulder trench or edge drain. These work with the flat and sloped shoulder stones to finish the edge and its drainage.

PP or ABS for shoulder and cap moulds?

Thin flat shoulder stones (8–10cm) demould well in PP for 300+ pour cycles. Thick bridge caps (up to 33.5cm high) and L-shaped corner caps with deep right-angle sections are better in ABS for stiffness and dimensional stability over heavy pours. We recommend material based on the section depth.

What concrete grade and reinforcement do these need?

C25–C30 concrete with vibration and curing for standard shoulder stones. Bridge caps and tall retaining blocks that distribute pier or backfill load should use higher-grade reinforced concrete per the bridge design — the mould accommodates rebar cages. Note that load capacity is a property of the concrete, reinforcement and section, not of the mould.

Mixed-SKU orders are welcome with no strict MOQ on catalogue sizes. Custom sections, bevels, hole layouts or L-shaped caps are made to order, typically 15–30 days after drawing approval.
