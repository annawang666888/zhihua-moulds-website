Kerbstone & Curbstone Mould Buyer Guide

Kerbstone mould orders are usually driven by road authority profiles, project drawings and whether straight, slope, L-type, S-type or radius pieces are required.

Typical kerb mould types

  • Straight kerbstone moulds: common road edge and sidewalk boundary components.
  • L-type and slope curbstone moulds: used where a curb also needs water guidance or edge protection.
  • S-type and decorative side stone moulds: useful for parks, communities and landscaping boundaries.
  • Radius kerb and corner moulds: important for road turns, medians, parking islands and roundabout edges.

Project drawing checklist

For kerbstone moulds, a side profile drawing is more useful than a general product name. The slope, chamfer, base width, visible height and curve radius all affect mould structure.

  • Prepare cross-section profile and finished kerb length.
  • Mark the visible face, ground-buried part and water-flow side.
  • For curved kerbs, provide radius, angle and quantity by each curve type.

What to send for a fast quote

Kerbstone projects often follow local road authority profiles. A cross-section drawing or clear product photo helps us confirm slope, radius, chamfer and demoulding direction.

  • Finished kerb length, width, height and profile drawing
  • Whether it is L-type, S-type, slope, straight, radius or corner type
  • Required radius or turning angle for curved sections
  • Application: road edge, median, sidewalk, parking or landscaping
  • Quantity, destination country and mixed-profile requirements

Kerbstone Mould FAQ

Can you make kerbstone moulds from local drawings?

Yes. Send a cross-section drawing, dimensions or sample photo. We can review the profile and suggest a mould structure.

Can I order straight and radius kerbs together?

Yes. Mixed-SKU orders are common for road projects where straight kerbs, curved kerbs and corner pieces are needed together.

Are these moulds only for road projects?

No. Kerbstone moulds are also used for landscaping edges, parks, residential communities, parking lots and public spaces.
