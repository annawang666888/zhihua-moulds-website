What to send for a fast quote

Power utility components often need project-specific dimensions and markings. Please send drawings or clear photos for faster review.

  • Finished component size and intended use: cable well, cover, coping, marker or trench part
  • Load or installation environment: sidewalk, road edge, substation or industrial area
  • Marking text, logo, lifting hole, rib or anti-slip pattern requirements
  • Concrete surface finish and demoulding method requirements
  • Quantity, destination country and project schedule

Power Engineering Moulds FAQ

Can you make custom moulds for power utility projects?

Yes. Send drawings, dimensions or sample photos of the concrete component. We can review whether an existing mould fits or whether an OEM mould is needed.

Can these moulds be supplied with cable trench moulds together?

Yes. Power engineering components, cable trench moulds and cover moulds can be mixed in one shipment where packing allows.

Do you provide fixed public prices for these items?

Most power engineering moulds are quoted by size, structure, material and order quantity, so we provide project quotations rather than public fixed prices.
