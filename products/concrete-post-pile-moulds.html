Concrete Post & Marker Pile Mould Buyer Guide

Fence posts, landscape wood-grain piles, railway cable marker piles and wire-mesh boundary posts — match the mould to the post length, section and embossed marking.

Four mould groups on this page

  • Cylindrical columns: round moulds Ø20–21cm, 41–51cm high for short bollards and columns.
  • Imitation-wood piles: Ø15/20/25/30cm, 50–150cm long; smooth, wood-grain or tree-grain; pointed or flat; matching wood-grain caps for Ø15/20/30.
  • Square posts: 110–260cm long, 10×10 to 25×25cm sections; includes ABS two-section and spliced long posts.
  • Marker & wire-mesh posts: plain/triangular marker piles, railway cable marker piles, and wire-mesh fence posts up to 290cm incl. "No Trespassing".

Three things buyers get wrong

  • Ordering a very long post as one mould. Posts over ~2.4m use spliced or two-section moulds — confirm construction so demoulding and joining are planned.
  • Forgetting to specify embossed text. Marker-pile wording (cable warning, "No Trespassing", unit name) must be engraved before the mould is cut; it cannot be added to a finished plain pile.
  • Mixing pile and cap diameters. The wood-grain cap must match the pile diameter (Ø15/20/30cm); a cap for one size will not seat on another.

Concrete Post, Pile & Marker Mould FAQ

What kinds of concrete post and pile moulds are on this page?

Four families: (1) cylindrical round-column moulds (Ø20–21cm, 41–51cm high) for short bollards and columns; (2) imitation-wood landscape pile moulds (仿木桩) in Ø15/20/25/30cm and 50–150cm lengths, smooth, wood-grain or tree-grain, pointed or flat, with matching wood-grain pile caps; (3) square section post moulds from 110 to 260cm long, including an ABS two-section 250×25×25 post and a spliced 260cm post; and (4) marker piles and wire-mesh fence posts — plain or triangular marker piles, railway optical/electric cable marker piles, and wire-mesh fence posts up to 290cm including the 'No Trespassing' (禁止入内) boundary post.

How are long fence posts moulded — can a 2.9m post come from one mould?

Very long posts use split or spliced moulds. The 260×13.2×10.3cm post is a spliced (拼接) mould and the 250×25×25cm post is a two-section (两段组合) ABS mould; sections align and are cast together or joined per the design. Posts from 110 to 240cm are single-piece. Tell us the target post length and section; we will confirm single-piece vs spliced construction, which affects demoulding and shipping.

Can marker piles be made with custom embossed text or symbols?

Yes. Marker piles (标志桩) commonly carry warning text, unit names or symbols — for example the railway optical/electric cable marker pile (铁路光缆电缆标志桩) and the 'No Trespassing' (禁止入内) wire-mesh post. Send the required wording, language and logo; the text is engraved into the mould so every cast part carries it. Plain unmarked (无字) marker piles are also standard.

What is the difference between smooth, wood-grain and pointed imitation-wood piles?

Smooth (光面) piles have a plain concrete surface; wood-grain (木纹) or tree-grain (树纹) piles are cast from a textured mould that looks like timber for landscape and waterfront use; pointed (尖头) piles have a tapered driving end for garden edging, revetment and flower-bed borders. Half-grain versions are smooth on one side and grained on the other. Matching wood-grain pile caps (木纹盖) cap the top for Ø15/20/30cm piles.

Do the imitation-wood piles need the separate pile cap mould?

The wood-grain cap (仿木桩木纹盖, e.g. 22×19×1.5, 27.5×24×2 or 37.5×34.5×2.5cm) is a separate mould that forms a decorative/driving cap sized to the pile diameter. Order the cap mould in the same diameter as the pile (Ø15/20/30cm). Pointed driving piles usually do not use a cap; flat-topped landscape piles do.

PP or ABS for these post moulds?

Standard square and round posts demould well in PP for 300+ pour cycles. ABS is used for large or complex sections — the 250×25×25cm two-section post and posts with deep engraved text — where stiffer walls hold dimension over repeated pours. We recommend the material based on the section and your volume.

What concrete and reinforcement do fence and marker posts need?

C25–C30 concrete with vibration and standard curing. Fence and wire-mesh posts that take mesh or rail loads, and long slender posts (10×10 or 12×12cm sections over 2m), should include rebar or pre-stressed wire; the mould suits reinforced concrete. Marker piles are usually unreinforced or lightly reinforced for roadside burial.

What is the MOQ, lead time and how are long posts shipped?

Mixed-SKU orders are welcome with no strict MOQ on catalogue posts. Custom-text marker piles and spliced/long posts are made to order, typically 15–30 days after drawing approval. Long post moulds load best in 40'HQ; we plan container loading around the longest piece. Send drawings, quantities, wording and destination port for an FOB Ningbo/Shanghai quote.
