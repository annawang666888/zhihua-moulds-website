Striped Drainage Cover Mould Buyer Guide

How to specify striped concrete drain covers for pedestrian and landscape drainage before requesting a quotation.

Where striped covers are used

  • Pedestrian and park drainage: the raised stripe pattern gives a directional walking surface on covers that sit flush with paving.
  • Plaza and streetscape trench runs: long covers laid end to end read as a continuous linear feature rather than separate lids.
  • Notched variants: a notch on one or both ends lets the cover seat into a channel shoulder instead of resting on top.
  • Common sizes in this range: 44.4×49.4×6cm, 49.4×44.4×6cm, 59×50×10cm, 73×49×10cm and 79.4×49.4×6cm.

Specification notes

Striped covers are cast face-down, so the stripe geometry sits in the base of the mould. Deeper, narrower stripes look sharper but need more vibration to fill and more draft to release cleanly. Send the channel opening size first — the cover has to match the channel, not the other way round.

  • Give the finished concrete size, not the mould outside size.
  • State whether the cover needs an end notch and on how many sides.
  • Say if you cast with a steel identification plate — the pattern layout has to leave room for it.

How the Anti-Slip Ribbed Face Is Formed

A ribbed anti-slip cover mould turned over so you can see the full cavity. The grooves are cast in the same pour as the slab — nothing is cut or treated afterwards. Groove depth, direction and draft angle decide whether the cover demoulds cleanly and still grips underfoot. Tell us your cover size and we will match a ribbed pattern to it.

FAQ

Are striped drainage cover moulds only decorative?

No. They are functional concrete drainage covers with a striped surface pattern. The pattern improves appearance and can add slip resistance.

Can you customize the cover size and hole pattern?

Yes. Send the cover size, load requirement, hole or slot drawing and sample photo for review.

Which buyers need this mould type?

Precast factories, landscape contractors, municipal suppliers and drainage channel buyers looking for a finished cover surface.
