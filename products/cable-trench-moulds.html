Cable Trench & Utility Duct Mould Buyer Guide

Cable trench moulds should be selected by trench body size, cover structure, installation environment and whether the project needs matching utility cover moulds.

Common cable trench mould choices

  • U-shaped cable trench moulds: open concrete cable channels for power, telecom and industrial utility lines.
  • Deep utility trench moulds: larger components for cable protection, inspection and repeated installation dimensions.
  • Trench cover moulds: matching covers with holes, ribs or anti-slip patterns.
  • Double-slot duct moulds: used when different cable or service lines need separation.

Engineering details that matter

For cable trench moulds, small details such as cover grooves, lifting holes, drainage holes and wall thickness affect whether the finished concrete part fits the site installation.

  • Provide inside and outside dimensions if available.
  • Confirm whether the trench needs a separate cover mould.
  • Tell us if the product is for power, telecom, railway, substation or municipal corridor use.

Cable Trench Moulds FAQ

Can you make cable trench moulds from local project drawings?

Yes. Send the finished trench drawing, section profile, cover requirement and any installation details. We can review standard catalogue options or OEM mould feasibility.

Can one order include trench body and cover moulds?

Yes. Mixed orders for U-shaped cable trench bodies and matching cover moulds are common for utility projects.

Which details affect cable trench mould quotation most?

The finished trench size, cover structure, wall thickness, ribs, hole design, mould material requirement and order quantity have the biggest effect on quotation.
