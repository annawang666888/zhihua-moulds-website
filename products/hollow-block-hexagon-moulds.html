Moulds for Slope Protection and River Bank Revetment

Hollow hexagonal revetment blocks are the open-cored units laid across embankments, river banks, canal slopes and highway cuttings. The hexagon interlocks on six sides so the mat stays keyed together under water flow, while the open core lets grass root through and turns the slope into vegetated erosion control.

Zhihua supplies 54 hollow hexagonal block mould sizes from 14.5cm to 25cm side length, with wall thickness specified from 3cm up to 8.7cm and tapered wall options for easier demoulding. The range also covers plum-blossom and inner-circle patterns, three-hole units, semi-hollow blocks and half-hexagon moulds for finishing the mat edge.

Hexagonal Revetment Block Mould Buyer Guide

Revetment block moulds are specified as side length × block height, plus wall thickness. Wall thickness is the durability decision and is usually tapered so blocks release cleanly from the mould.

How to specify a revetment block mould

  • Side length 14.5–18cm: lighter mats for canal linings, garden slopes and low-flow banks.
  • Side length 20cm: the most widely used revetment size — 25 designs available with heights from 6cm to 30cm.
  • Side length 22–25cm: heavy revetment for main river banks and high-flow channels.
  • Wall thickness 3–8.7cm: quoted as a taper such as 6–5cm, meaning 6cm at the wide face and 5cm at the narrow face for clean demoulding.

Project drawing checklist

For revetment block moulds, wall thickness and block height together decide concrete volume per square metre. Send the slope drawing or design specification and we can match the closest catalogue mould.

  • Confirm side length and block height (e.g. 20×15cm).
  • State required wall thickness, including taper if specified (e.g. 6–5cm).
  • Tell us whether the core is planted with grass or filled with gravel.

Hollow Hexagon Block Mould FAQ

What is a hexagonal revetment block used for?

Hollow hexagonal revetment blocks are laid across river banks, canal slopes and highway embankments. They interlock on six sides to resist water flow, and their open cores are planted with grass or filled with gravel so the slope becomes vegetated erosion control.

What does wall thickness 6–5cm mean?

It is a tapered wall: 6cm at the wide face and 5cm at the narrow face. The taper lets the cast block release cleanly from the mould and is standard practice on hollow revetment blocks.

How do I choose the right revetment block size?

Side length 14.5–18cm suits canal linings and low-flow banks, 20cm is the most widely used size for river banks and embankments, and 22–25cm is specified for high-flow channels and main river works. Block height then follows the design concrete volume.

Do you supply half-hexagon blocks for the mat edge?

Yes. We supply hollow half-hexagon and semi-hollow moulds in 15×20, 17×8, 20×6, 20×8, 20×12, 20×15, 20×40×12 and 25×20cm so the revetment mat can be finished at the edge without cutting blocks.
