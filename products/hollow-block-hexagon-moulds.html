A practical bridge between paver, block and slope-protection demand

Hollow block and hexagon moulds match two high-value overseas needs: standardized concrete block/paver production and infrastructure slope-protection or revetment projects. They are useful for sidewalks, landscaping, water conservancy, embankments and ecological protection works.

This category supports hollow concrete blocks, multi-hole blocks, hollow hexagonal slope blocks and flat hexagonal paving slabs. Buyers can combine these moulds with paver block, slope protection and kerbstone product lines.

FAQ

Are hollow hexagon moulds used for slope protection?

Yes. Hollow hexagonal concrete blocks are often used for slope protection, revetment, river banks, channels and ecological erosion-control projects.

Can these moulds be sold together with paver moulds?

Yes. Flat hexagon and hollow block moulds fit paver/block buyers, while hollow hexagon products also connect to slope-protection buyers.

Can Zhihua make custom hole size or wall thickness?

Yes. Provide the finished product dimensions, hole layout, wall thickness and application. We can review whether ABS, PP or PE is more suitable.
