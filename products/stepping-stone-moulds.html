Moulds for Garden Paths, Patios and Landscape Paving

Stepping stone and faux stone paving moulds are used to cast the decorative concrete units that form garden paths, patios, courtyard walkways and landscape features. Because the mould face carries the finished texture, pattern definition and demoulding quality directly decide how the paving looks.

Zhihua supplies moulds for round and rectangular stepping stones, faux stone slabs, matte litchi-texture stone, wood-grain walkway and strip stones, plus traditional Chinese lotus, cloud, fret and blessing pattern tiles. Standard catalogue sizes and OEM patterns are both available.

Stepping Stone Mould Buyer Guide

Stepping stone mould orders are usually driven by the surface pattern, finished slab size and thickness, plus whether the paving is walked on directly or set into gravel or lawn.

Typical stepping stone mould types

  • Round stepping stones: Ø35–50cm discs for lawn and gravel paths, cast 5–6cm thick.
  • Faux stone slabs: irregular stone texture in 5×5cm up to 100×45cm, including 1-out-N multi-cavity moulds.
  • Wood-grain walkway stones: 90–200cm long strips imitating timber decking and sleepers.
  • Patterned garden tiles: lotus, cloud, fret, coin and blessing motifs for courtyards and temple paving.

Project drawing checklist

For stepping stone and paving tile moulds, the key parameters are the finished slab dimensions, thickness and surface pattern. A photo of the target paving helps us match the texture.

  • Confirm finished slab length × width × thickness.
  • Send a photo or sketch of the required surface pattern or texture.
  • State whether the units are laid on sand, gravel, lawn or mortar bed.

What to send for a fast quote

Stepping stone and paving tile projects usually start from a target look. A photo of the paving plus finished dimensions lets us match a catalogue mould or quote an OEM pattern.

  • Finished slab dimensions and thickness
  • Required surface pattern or texture (photo helps)
  • Whether round, rectangular, strip or multi-cavity is preferred
  • Quantity, destination country and mixed-pattern requirements

Stepping Stone Mould FAQ

What sizes of stepping stone moulds do you supply?

Standard round stepping stones are Ø20cm, Ø35cm and 50cm, cast 5–6cm thick. Rectangular and faux stone slabs run from 5×5cm multi-cavity moulds up to 200×15cm wood-grain strips. Custom sizes can be quoted from drawings.

Can you make a mould from a photo of the paving I want?

Yes. Send a clear photo of the surface texture or pattern. We can review whether the texture can be reproduced and quote OEM mould production.

How many times can one stepping stone mould be used?

Typical service life is 300+ pour cycles with normal demoulding care and release agent use.
