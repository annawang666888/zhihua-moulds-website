Concrete counterweight blocks for solar mounting projects

Photovoltaic ballast blocks are used as precast concrete support or counterweight components for solar mounting systems. Consistent mould dimensions help contractors produce repeatable blocks for project installation.

Zhihua supports grooved ballast blocks, plain PV concrete blocks and project-specific solar support-base moulds from drawings or sample photos.

ApplicationsGround-mounted solar supports, rooftop ballast systems, distributed PV bases, temporary solar mounts and project counterweight blocks.

Photovoltaic Ballast Block Moulds FAQ

What are photovoltaic ballast block moulds used for?

They are used to precast concrete counterweight or support-base blocks for photovoltaic mounting systems, especially where repeatable block sizes are needed.

Can the groove or block size be customized?

Yes. Groove size, block length, width, height, support slot and other details can be reviewed from drawings or sample photos.

What information should I send for quotation?

Please send finished block dimensions, groove or slot detail, installation scenario, quantity, destination country and any project drawing or sample photo.
