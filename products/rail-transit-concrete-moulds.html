What to send for a fast quote

Rail transit projects usually follow drawings or local standards. Send the finished component drawing, application and quantity for faster review.

  • Finished component size and section drawing
  • Component type: fence beam, railing section, roadbed protection component, utility part or custom precast component
  • Installation environment: railway line, metro station, maintenance corridor or utility section
  • Marking, logo, letters, lifting hole, groove, rib or embedded-part requirements
  • Quantity, destination country and project schedule

Rail Transit Concrete Moulds FAQ

Can you make railway concrete moulds from project drawings?

Yes. Send drawings, finished dimensions, section profiles and sample photos. We can review whether catalogue moulds fit or OEM moulds are needed.

Can rail transit moulds be supplied with related infrastructure moulds?

Yes. Rail transit fence, railing, roadbed protection and related power engineering components can be planned together where packing and order quantity allow.

Can logos, letters or project markings be customized?

Yes. Logo, letters, warning marks or project identification patterns can be customized when the mould design and demoulding direction allow.
