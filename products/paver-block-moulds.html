Paver Block Mould Buyer Guide

Choose paver moulds by application, finished thickness, surface texture and local-market pattern demand.

Common paver mould families

  • Interlocking paver block moulds: practical for sidewalks, plazas, courtyards and light traffic areas.
  • Paving slab moulds: square, rectangular and decorative slabs for parks, gardens and public spaces.
  • Grass paver moulds: open-cell pavers for green parking, landscaping and permeable pavement projects.
  • Decorative stone moulds: textured or patterned moulds for local retail and landscaping markets.

How to avoid wrong mould selection

The same surface pattern can perform very differently at 2.5 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm or 6 cm thickness. Before ordering, confirm the final use environment and whether the paver is mainly decorative, pedestrian-use or driveway-use.

  • Send a clear top-view photo when copying a popular local paver.
  • Confirm finished thickness and edge chamfer before quoting.
  • For coloured concrete pavers, ask whether surface texture may trap pigment residue.

What to send for a fast quote

For paving products, the pattern and finished product thickness are key. A photo of a local popular paver can be enough for an initial feasibility review.

  • Finished paver length, width and thickness
  • Surface texture, pattern or sample photo
  • Expected production material: PP, ABS or recommendation needed
  • Application: pedestrian, driveway, plaza, grass parking or garden use
  • Quantity, destination country and whether mixed product styles are needed

Paver Block Mould FAQ

Can these moulds produce coloured pavers?

Yes. These moulds can be used with pigment-coloured concrete for common red, yellow, grey, black and decorative paving products.

Can I order several paving patterns together?

Yes. Mixed-SKU orders are common for paver and landscaping products, especially when customers test multiple local-market patterns.

Can you copy a local paving stone pattern?

We can review a sample photo, dimensions or drawing and suggest whether a practical plastic mould structure can be made.
