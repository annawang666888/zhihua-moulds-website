Moulds for Decorative Screen Walls and Ventilated Facades

Breeze blocks — also called decorative screen blocks, ventilation blocks or jali blocks — are perforated concrete units used to build screen walls, privacy partitions, ventilated facades, garden walls and balcony infills. They provide shade and airflow while keeping a strong architectural pattern.

Zhihua supplies moulds for 48 pattern designs across 20×20×6cm, 25×25×6cm, 30×30×7cm, 32×32×10cm, 34×34×6cm and 40×40×7cm formats, including three-in-one mini blocks and framed decorative units. OEM patterns can be produced from drawings or sample photos.

Breeze Block Mould Buyer Guide

Breeze block mould selection is driven by the block face size, thickness and pattern, plus whether the wall is load-bearing infill or purely a decorative screen.

Typical breeze block formats

  • 20×20×6cm: the most widely used screen block size, 26 pattern designs available.
  • 30×30×7cm: larger face for feature walls and faster wall build-up, 18 designs available.
  • 25×25 – 34×34cm: intermediate formats including 32×32×10cm thick blocks and square fret patterns.
  • 40×40×7cm: large-format screen block for high walls and facade panels.

Project drawing checklist

For breeze block moulds, the key parameters are block face dimensions, thickness and the pattern opening geometry. A photo of the reference wall helps us match the design.

  • Confirm block face size and thickness (e.g. 20×20×6cm).
  • Send a photo or drawing of the required pattern.
  • State whether blocks are stacked dry, mortared or reinforced.

What to send for a fast quote

Breeze block projects are usually driven by an architectural reference image. A photo of the target wall plus block dimensions lets us match a catalogue pattern or quote an OEM mould.

  • Block face dimensions and thickness
  • Pattern reference photo, drawing or catalogue number
  • Wall area in square metres and required block quantity
  • Destination country and whether mixed patterns are needed

Breeze Block Mould FAQ

What is a breeze block used for?

Breeze blocks are perforated concrete units used for screen walls, privacy partitions, ventilated facades, garden walls and balcony infills. They allow airflow and light while forming a decorative pattern.

How many breeze block patterns do you have?

We currently offer 48 pattern designs across 20×20, 25×25, 30×30, 32×32, 34×34 and 40×40cm formats. Additional patterns can be produced as OEM moulds.

Can you make a custom breeze block pattern?

Yes. Send a drawing, CAD file or photo of the pattern. We can review mould feasibility, draft angles and demoulding direction, then quote OEM production.
