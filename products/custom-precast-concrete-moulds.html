Custom moulds for project-specific precast concrete components

Not every infrastructure project uses catalogue dimensions. Local standards, municipal drawings, contractor requirements and customer logos often require a custom mould instead of a standard product.

Zhihua supports custom precast concrete mould development from drawings, dimensions and sample photos. Common material options include ABS, PP and PE. Production lead time depends on mould size, structure, material, quantity and production schedule, so it is confirmed case by case.

What to send for a fast quote

For custom moulds, the more accurate the drawing and application details, the faster we can review feasibility and quotation direction.

  • Finished concrete product dimensions and section drawing
  • Clear sample photos from multiple angles if no drawing is available
  • Application and installation environment
  • Estimated quantity and target country
  • Preferred material if known: ABS, PP or PE
  • Logo, letters, surface pattern, marking or local-standard details

Custom Precast Concrete Moulds FAQ

Do you support custom moulds from drawings?

Yes. Zhihua supports custom moulds from drawings, dimensions and sample photos.

What should customers provide for OEM mould review?

Please provide size, drawing, sample photo, application, quantity, target country and any material, logo, letter or pattern requirements.

Which materials are commonly used?

The main custom plastic mould materials are ABS, PP and PE.

Can you make logo, letters or patterns on the concrete product?

Yes. Logo, letters and patterns can be customized if the mould structure and demoulding direction allow.

What is the production lead time?

The exact production cycle is not fixed yet. It depends on product size, mould structure, material, quantity and current production schedule, so it is confirmed after review.
