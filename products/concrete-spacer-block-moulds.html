Injection-moulded rebar spacer moulds built for cover accuracy & 300+ pours

Concrete spacer block moulds produce the small elements that sit between rebar and formwork to hold the specified concrete cover thickness. The construction-industry standard for these moulds is injection moulding: molten reinforced polypropylene (PP) or ABS is forced under high pressure into a precision-machined steel cavity, producing a rigid, dimensionally consistent mould with smooth demoulding walls that survives 300+ concrete pour cycles.

Zhihua runs the injection moulding in-house on its own tool shop, so every rebar spacer mould — whether a square/conical/sloped spacer block, a saddle spacer, a bar strip spacer, a plum-blossom rebar cover, a round disc cover or a slot insert — uses a steel tool cut from the buyer's drawing or sample. Standard catalogue items and OEM dimensions are both available.

Buyers searching for injection moulding for rebar spacer, plastic rebar spacer moulds, concrete spacer mould or cover block mould are usually comparing three things: cover-thickness accuracy, 1-out-N count (pieces per pour), and mould service life. All three are locked in at the tool-cutting stage — see the Concrete Spacer Block Moulds Buying Guide for a project checklist.

Concrete Spacer Block Mould Buyer Guide

Spacer block mould orders are usually driven by rebar cover thickness, mould 1-out-N count and whether the mould is for sloped, saddle, bar strip, plum-blossom or round disc applications.

Typical spacer mould types

  • Spacer block moulds: square, conical, sloped and U-shaped blocks for general cover.
  • Saddle spacers: used in slab-on-grade and foundation work to support rebar mesh.
  • Bar strip and plum-blossom spacers: for narrow rebar or specific cover thickness requirements.
  • Round disc and slot insert spacers: for vertical rebar in columns and walls.

Project drawing checklist

For spacer block moulds, the key parameters are the cover thickness, finished block dimensions and the 1-out-N count. A sample photo or marked drawing helps confirm orientation and demoulding direction.

  • Specify the required cover thickness (e.g. 20mm, 30mm, 50mm).
  • Confirm the finished spacer dimensions and 1-out-N count.
  • Mark whether the mould is for horizontal slab or vertical rebar use.

Concrete Rebar Spacer Mould FAQ

Are these rebar spacer moulds injection moulded?

Yes. All Zhihua spacer and cover-block moulds are injection moulded from reinforced polypropylene (PP) or ABS on in-house injection moulding machines using our own steel tools. This is the same process used by major rebar spacer brands worldwide and is what gives the moulds their 300+ pour cycle rating and consistent cover thickness.

Why injection moulding instead of other plastic processes for rebar spacers?

Injection moulding produces a one-piece, void-free wall with exact cover-thickness tolerance and a smooth interior for clean demoulding. It also allows 1-out-N tools (16/25/36/42/100 cavities per pour), which is the main lever on spacer piece-cost. Thermoformed or 3D-printed moulds cannot match this on either cycle count or dimensional consistency.

What plastic is used for concrete spacer moulds?

Reinforced polypropylene (PP) is standard for most spacer and cover block moulds because it flexes slightly for demoulding and withstands concrete alkalinity across 300+ cycles. ABS is used for thinner-wall, higher-precision profiles such as small round-disc and slot-insert spacers. We specify the material per profile at tool-cutting.

Can you make custom spacer moulds from drawings?

Yes. Send a sample photo, dimensions or marked drawing. We cut the steel injection-mould tool in-house and typically ship first-article samples in 15–30 days after drawing approval.

Can I order different spacer types in one shipment?

Yes. Mixed-SKU orders are common for construction projects where multiple spacer types are required together.

Are these moulds only for rebar cover?

No. Spacer block moulds are also used for ballast blocks, concrete test specimens and other precast concrete elements.
