Focused cover slab moulds for drainage and municipal projects

Dot pattern drainage cover moulds are suitable when buyers need a textured concrete cover surface that improves foot grip and gives the slab a clean municipal appearance.

These moulds can be used by precast factories, road contractors and municipal suppliers that need repeatable concrete cover slabs for drainage channels, trenches, sidewalks, utility corridors and landscaping areas.

What to send for a fast quote

For cover slab and drainage cover moulds, the finished concrete size, cover thickness and surface pattern are important. If you already have a local sample, a clear photo with measurements is enough for the first review.

  • Finished cover slab length, width, height/thickness and edge detail
  • Surface pattern: diamond, dot, slot, anti-slip or custom texture
  • Expected application: road shoulder, drainage trench, sidewalk, yard or municipal project
  • Quantity, destination country and preferred packing or container plan
  • Whether logo, letters, numbering or local-standard marks are required

Dot Cover Mould FAQ

Can you make different surface patterns?

Yes. Diamond, dot, slot, anti-slip, logo and lettering patterns can be reviewed from drawings or sample photos.

Can this be shipped with other drainage moulds?

Yes. Cover slab moulds are often shipped together with U-drain, cable trench, manhole cover or kerbstone moulds for municipal project buyers.

Why keep the full catalogue image instead of cropping only the product?

Keeping the full card helps buyers see the mould image, Chinese title, dimensions and orange application notes. This reduces misunderstanding during early quotation discussion.
