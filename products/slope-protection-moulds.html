Slope Protection Mould Buyer Guide

Slope protection mould selection starts from the finished block face size and the slope gradient, not from the mould price. The 21 sizes on this page span 29–59 cm long, 15–44 cm wide and 5–24 cm thick.

Block types on this page

  • Ecological grass-planting blocks: open-cell blocks that let vegetation root through the slope face. Most common family, 29–50 cm formats.
  • River channel blocks: heavy 24 cm-thick sections with 3–4 cm drainage holes for canal and river-bank lining.
  • Hydraulic capping blocks: tapered top-of-slope closing pieces (45 × 36.9 × 10 cm, top face 35 cm).
  • Companion block sets: smaller infill units laid together with a main block to close out a bonding pattern.

Three things buyers get wrong

  • Thickness is a structural choice, not a cost option. On this page thickness runs 5–24 cm for a similar face size. A thinner block is cheaper per piece but is specified for gentler slopes and lower flow velocity — match it to the design, not to the budget.
  • A quoted size like 16.2–15 cm is draft taper, not a tolerance. The block is 16.2 cm at one face and 15 cm at the other so it releases from the mould. Do not read it as "±0.6 cm".
  • Grass-planting void ratio drives the vegetation result. Two blocks with the same outer size can differ from roughly 25% to 35% open area. Ask for the void ratio, not just the block dimensions.

Slope Protection Moulds FAQ

Can you make slope protection moulds from drawings?

Yes. Send the finished concrete block drawing, sample photo, size and application. We can review catalogue options or OEM mould feasibility.

Can logo, letters or patterns be made on the slope block?

Yes. Logos, letters, warning marks, surface patterns and project-specific details can be customized when the mould structure allows.

Which materials are commonly used?

For Zhihua custom plastic moulds, ABS, PP and PE are the main material options. The suitable material depends on product shape, quantity and usage method.
