Built for river banks, canals and road embankment protection

Slope protection moulds are used to precast concrete blocks for erosion control, river-bank reinforcement, canal lining and road embankment stabilization. They are often used together with drainage channel, kerbstone and utility infrastructure products.

Zhihua supplies plastic moulds for hexagonal slope blocks, hollow ecological blocks, interlocking revetment blocks and custom project profiles from drawings or sample photos.

ApplicationsRiver banks, canals, irrigation channels, road embankments, highway side slopes, drainage ditches, reservoir slopes and ecological restoration projects
Product scopeHexagonal slope block moulds, hollow revetment block moulds, interlocking slope protection moulds, river-bank concrete block moulds and ecological grass-planting block moulds
CustomizationBlock shape, side length, thickness, hollow opening, interlocking joint, surface pattern, logo/letters and local project dimensions

What to send for a fast quote

Slope Protection Moulds FAQ

Can you make slope protection moulds from drawings?

Yes. Send the finished concrete block drawing, sample photo, size and application. We can review catalogue options or OEM mould feasibility.

Can logo, letters or patterns be made on the slope block?

Yes. Logos, letters, warning marks, surface patterns and project-specific details can be customized when the mould structure allows.

Which materials are commonly used?

For Zhihua custom plastic moulds, ABS, PP and PE are the main material options. The suitable material depends on product shape, quantity and usage method.
