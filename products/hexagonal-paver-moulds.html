Moulds for Hexagonal Paving and Walkway Slabs

Hexagonal pavers interlock on six sides, which spreads wheel load better than square slabs and stops the paving creeping under traffic. That makes hexagon paving slabs a standard choice for pavements, parking aprons, park walkways and water-conservancy revetment surfaces.

Zhihua supplies 60 hexagonal paver mould sizes measured as side length × thickness, from 8×5cm multi-cavity moulds up to 30×15cm heavy sections. The range covers flat, solid, concave, concave-base, convex, anti-slip and interlocking faces, plus matching half-hexagon moulds for finishing paving edges without cutting.

Hexagonal Paver Mould Buyer Guide

Hexagon paver moulds are specified as side length × thickness. Thickness is the load decision: 6cm for footpaths, 8–10cm for light vehicles, 12cm and above for heavy or hydraulic works.

How to choose thickness and face type

  • 6cm thickness: footpaths, garden paths and pedestrian-only paving.
  • 8–10cm thickness: car parks, driveways and light vehicle traffic — the most ordered range.
  • 12–15cm thickness: heavy vehicle aprons, slope surfaces and water-conservancy revetment.
  • Anti-slip, concave & convex faces: anti-slip and concave-base types are specified for wet slopes and drainage surfaces; recess depths of 1cm and 2cm are available.

Project drawing checklist

For hexagonal paver moulds, confirm the side length rather than the across-corners dimension — a 20cm hexagon paver measures 40cm across the diagonal, which is a common source of ordering errors.

  • Confirm side length and thickness (e.g. 20×8cm side × thickness).
  • State expected traffic load: pedestrian, light vehicle or heavy vehicle.
  • Add matching half-hexagon moulds if paving edges must be finished without cutting.

Hexagonal Paver Mould FAQ

How are hexagonal paver moulds measured?

Hexagonal pavers are specified as side length × thickness. A 20×8cm hexagon paver has 20cm sides and is 8cm thick, which measures about 40cm across the diagonal. Always confirm side length, not the across-corners dimension.

What thickness of hexagon paver should I choose?

Use 6cm for footpaths and garden paths, 8–10cm for car parks and light vehicle traffic, and 12–15cm for heavy vehicle aprons, slope surfaces and water-conservancy works.

Do you supply half-hexagon moulds for paving edges?

Yes. We supply matching flat, solid and anti-slip half-hexagon moulds in 17×8, 21×23×8, 25×6, 25×10, 25×12.5, 30×6 and 30×10cm so edges can be finished without cutting full pavers on site.

What is a 1-out-3 or 1-out-4 hexagon paver mould?

These are multi-cavity moulds that cast three or four small pavers in a single pour. Our 8×5cm 1-out-4 and 9×5cm 1-out-3 moulds are used for small-format decorative paving where labour per unit matters.
