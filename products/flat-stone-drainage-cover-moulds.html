Flat Stone and Notched Cover Mould Buyer Guide

Flat-top covers and notched-seat covers for ditch lines, road shoulders and landscape drainage.

Two families on this page

  • Flat stone covers: a plain smooth top face. Chosen where the cover should disappear into the paving instead of adding texture.
  • Notched covers: a cut-out on one or both ends so the slab drops into the channel shoulder and sits flush.
  • Double-notched covers: notches at both ends, used on longer spans such as the 78×40×6cm size.
  • Sizes in this range: 30×30×8cm up to 78×40×6cm, with 40cm width being the most repeated.

Why the notch matters for pricing

A notch changes the mould, not just the concrete. The notch must have draft on every face or the cast slab locks in and the corner chips on release. Because of that, a notched version of the same nominal cover size is a different mould, not an option on an existing one.

  • Confirm notch dimensions in the format width × depth, e.g. 15×1.5cm.
  • State whether the top face is smooth, or needs a light broom or anti-slip finish.
  • For ditch lines, order the base slab and cover as a matched pair so the seat lines up.

FAQ

Are flat stone drainage cover moulds only decorative?

No. They are functional concrete drainage covers with a flat surface pattern. The pattern improves appearance and can add slip resistance.

Can you customize the cover size and hole pattern?

Yes. Send the cover size, load requirement, hole or slot drawing and sample photo for review.

Which buyers need this mould type?

Precast factories, landscape contractors, municipal suppliers and drainage channel buyers looking for a finished cover surface.
