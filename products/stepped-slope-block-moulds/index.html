Stepped Slope Block Moulds FAQ

What is a stepped slope block mould used for?

It is used to precast I-shaped or half-I concrete blocks for slope protection, river-bank revetment and embankment erosion control.

Can you make the mould from our drawing?

Yes. Send the finished block drawing, size, thickness and site application; we can review catalogue matching or OEM mould feasibility.

Can the block size be customized?

Yes. Length, width, thickness, interlocking details and surface patterns can be customized when the mould structure allows.
