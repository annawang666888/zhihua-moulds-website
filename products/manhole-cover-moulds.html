Manhole, Well Cover & Inspection Well Mould Buyer Guide

Use drawings or sample photos to separate cover moulds, frame moulds, well wall brick moulds and circular well-brick systems before quotation.

Common inspection-well product groups

  • Square and rectangular well covers: practical for sidewalks, communities, drainage points and small utility access.
  • Round sewer cover moulds: used for drainage and sewer access where a circular cover is required.
  • Inspection well wall brick moulds: modular bricks used to build circular or inspection-well walls.
  • Circular well brick systems: matched multi-piece sets for inner diameter requirements such as Ø90 cm or Ø110 cm.

Accuracy notes before ordering

For this category we avoid unsupported load-class claims unless the buyer provides the finished concrete design and test requirement. The quotation should focus on real dimensions, markings, frame fit and production method.

  • Confirm whether you need only the cover or both cover and frame.
  • Send logo, utility text and anti-slip pattern requirements as clear drawings.
  • For well bricks, provide inner diameter, wall thickness and number of pieces per ring.
