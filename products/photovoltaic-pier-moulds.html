Built for photovoltaic concrete base production

Photovoltaic pier and solar base moulds are used to produce repeatable concrete support or counterweight components for PV mounting projects. Accurate mould dimensions help keep installation alignment consistent across project quantities.

The verified image set for this batch supports photovoltaic ballast block moulds. Project-specific pile caps, support bases, grooves or slots can be reviewed from drawings before quotation.

What to send for a fast quote

Solar foundation projects require consistent base dimensions and support details. Send project drawings or a sample photo for quick feasibility review.

  • Finished base length × width × height and required groove/slot detail
  • Support type: ballast block, straight base, pile cap, cable-supported base or custom foundation
  • Installation environment: rooftop ballast, ground-mounted solar farm, distributed PV or utility-scale project
  • Embedded parts, lifting hole, reinforcement or cable channel requirements
  • Quantity, destination country and delivery schedule

Photovoltaic Pier & Solar Base Moulds FAQ

Can you make photovoltaic base moulds from project drawings?

Yes. Send the solar support base drawing, finished concrete dimensions and installation details. We can review mould structure and quotation.

Do you support grooved or plain PV ballast blocks?

Yes. Current verified examples include grooved and plain photovoltaic ballast block moulds in 30 × 20 × 30 cm and 30 × 30 × 30 cm styles.

Are these moulds suitable for repeatable solar project production?

Yes. Plastic moulds are suitable for repeatable precast production. The best option depends on quantity, concrete mix, demoulding method and required dimensional tolerance.
