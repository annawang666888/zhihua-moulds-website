Built for solar farm foundations and PV support bases

Photovoltaic pier moulds are used to produce repeatable concrete support bases for ground-mounted solar projects. Accurate mould dimensions help keep installation alignment consistent across large project quantities.

Zhihua supplies straight, grooved, double-slot and U-type solar support pier moulds, and can review OEM PV foundation components from project drawings or sample photos.

ApplicationsGround-mounted solar farms, photovoltaic support foundations, distributed PV bases, cable-supported solar pier systems and renewable-energy infrastructure projects
Product scopeStraight solar pier moulds, grooved PV base moulds, double-slot photovoltaic pier moulds, U-type support pier moulds and custom foundation components
CustomizationLength, width, height, groove/slot, embedded cable channel, support seat detail, lifting hole, reinforcement allowance and project drawing dimensions

What to send for a fast quote

Solar foundation projects require consistent base dimensions and support details. Send project drawings or a sample photo for quick feasibility review.

  • Finished pier length × width × height and required groove/slot detail
  • Support type: straight base, double-slot, U-type, cable-supported or custom foundation
  • Installation environment: ground-mounted solar farm, rooftop base, distributed PV or utility-scale project
  • Embedded parts, lifting hole, reinforcement or cable channel requirements
  • Quantity, destination country and delivery schedule

Photovoltaic Pier Moulds FAQ

Can you make photovoltaic pier moulds from project drawings?

Yes. Send the solar support base drawing, finished concrete dimensions and installation details. We can review mould structure and quotation.

Can the mould include cable grooves or support slots?

Yes. Grooves, slots, cable channels, support seats and lifting-hole details can be discussed from drawings or sample photos.

Are these moulds suitable for large solar farm quantities?

Yes. Plastic moulds are suitable for repeatable precast production. The best option depends on quantity, concrete mix, demoulding method and required dimensional tolerance.
