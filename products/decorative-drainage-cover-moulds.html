Patterned covers for drainage and landscape projects

Decorative drainage cover moulds are a strong extension of the drainage category. They serve the same stormwater and municipal demand while adding visible surface patterns for gardens, parks, sidewalks, residential communities and commercial plazas.

Zhihua can supply diamond, round-dot, stripe, cobblestone, flat-stone, notched and other cover patterns. These products cross-sell naturally with U-drain moulds, drainage channel moulds and paver moulds for overseas buyers.

What to send for a fast quote

For patterned drain covers, the key quote details are finished size, cover thickness, hole/slot design, surface pattern and whether the cover must fit an existing channel or frame.

  • Finished concrete product dimensions and clear drawing or sample photo
  • Expected mould material such as ABS, PP or PE
  • Quantity, destination country and target application
  • Surface pattern, logo, lettering or local standard requirements
  • Any special demoulding, stacking or packing requirement

FAQ

Are these covers only decorative?

No. They are practical concrete drainage covers with patterned surfaces. The pattern can improve appearance and help match landscape or sidewalk paving.

Can you customize the cover holes or slots?

Yes. Send the cover size, load/application, hole or slot drawing and sample photo. We can review whether the mould should be adjusted for demoulding.

Which buyers should consider this category?

Precast factories, garden contractors, municipal suppliers and drainage channel buyers who need concrete cover slabs with a more finished surface.
