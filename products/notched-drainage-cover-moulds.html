Notched Drainage Cover Mould Buyer Guide

Notched and recessed-face concrete covers for ditch lines, road shoulders and utility trenches.

Variants in this range

  • Notched covers: end cut-outs let the slab seat into the channel shoulder so the top sits flush with the surface.
  • Recessed-face covers: a sunken panel in the top face, cast to take a paving infill or reduce concrete volume.
  • Thick sections: this range runs to 12, 15 and 20cm thickness for vehicle-rated ditch lines, not only footway covers.
  • Sizes: from 25×24×12cm small trench lids up to 50cm-class covers, with notches typically 15-30cm wide × 1.5-3cm deep.

Getting the seat right

The notch is a seating detail, so it only works if the channel and the cover are quoted together. Give the channel shoulder width and the notch dimensions separately — a cover with a 22×3cm notch will not sit on a shoulder cast for a 15×1.5cm notch, even when the outside cover size is identical.

  • Quote notch as width × depth, and say how many ends carry it.
  • For recessed covers, give the recess depth and whether it takes a paving infill.
  • Thicker covers need more draft on the notch faces to demould without corner damage.

The mould cavity carries raised lettering, so the finished concrete cover comes out with your text or authority emblem recessed into the face — cast in one pour, no engraving or painting afterwards. Municipal and highway buyers usually need this. Send us the wording and we will build it into the tooling.

FAQ

Are notched drainage cover moulds only decorative?

No. They are functional concrete drainage covers with a notched surface pattern. The pattern improves appearance and can add slip resistance.

Can you customize the cover size and hole pattern?

Yes. Send the cover size, load requirement, hole or slot drawing and sample photo for review.

Which buyers need this mould type?

Precast factories, landscape contractors, municipal suppliers and drainage channel buyers looking for a finished cover surface.
