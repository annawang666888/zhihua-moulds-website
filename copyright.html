Copyright & Image Use Policy

How our product photographs, catalogue pages, drawings and videos may and may not be used — and how distributors can request authorization.

1. What is protected

All product photographs, factory photographs, catalogue layouts, dimension drawings, renderings, product videos and text on zhihuamoulds.com and in Zhihua Moulds catalogues are the copyrighted property of Jiaxing Zhihua Technology Co., Ltd. ("Zhihua Moulds"), Jiaxing, China, unless explicitly stated otherwise. Copyright has applied since the original creation date of each work.

2. Uses we do not authorize

  • Uploading our images or videos to B2B platforms (Alibaba, Made-in-China, Global Sources, IndiaMART, etc.), independent websites or social media to market third-party moulds or factories.
  • Removing, cropping out or covering watermarks, logos or company names.
  • Claiming our factory, workshop or shipment footage as another company's facility.
  • Redistributing or reselling our catalogues, or making them publicly downloadable from another website.
  • Using our images in paid advertising without written permission.

3. Uses we allow for our customers

  • Buyers of finished precast products may use photos of concrete items cast with Zhihua moulds in their own project or sales materials — please keep any watermark intact where present.
  • Authorized distributors and agents may use agreed product images and catalogue pages in their own marketing, subject to a simple written authorization defining scope, duration and attribution. Requests: anna@zhihuamoulds.com.
  • Press, media and trade publications may use single images with attribution "Image: Zhihua Moulds (zhihuamoulds.com)" and a link where technically possible.

4. Watermarked / client-issued catalogues

Catalogues issued to a named customer carry a per-recipient watermark ("Prepared for …"). Each licensed copy is traceable. Forwarding an issued catalogue to competitors, publishing it online, or using it outside its procurement purpose terminates that permission immediately.

5. Reporting infringement

If you have seen our images or videos used without authorization, send the page URL and a screenshot to anna@zhihuamoulds.com. We review reports promptly and enforce through the relevant channels, including Alibaba IPR complaints, platform takedown forms and Google DMCA removal requests. We keep original, date-stamped source files as evidence of ownership.

6. Trademarks

"Zhihua Moulds", the Zhihua logo and zhihuamoulds.com are trade identifiers of Jiaxing Zhihua Technology Co., Ltd. They may not be used to imply a relationship, endorsement or origin that does not exist.
