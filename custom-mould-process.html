OEM Process

Custom Mould Process from Drawing to Shipment

A clear OEM workflow for overseas buyers who need plastic moulds for non-standard precast concrete parts, local road profiles or project-specific components.

01

Send drawing, dimensions or sample photos

Share the finished concrete product size, cross-section drawing, application, target quantity and destination country. If no drawing is available, send clear photos from multiple angles with a ruler or reference size.

02

Feasibility review

We check whether the shape is suitable for plastic mould production, including demoulding direction, wall thickness, corners, surface patterns, logo/marking and expected concrete use.

03

Material and quotation discussion

ABS, PP and PE options are reviewed according to product size, use frequency, rigidity, weight and target cost. For mixed orders, several mould types can be discussed together.

04

Mould design and production

After confirmation, the mould structure is arranged for production. For complex OEM profiles, details such as chamfer, reinforcement, letter height and sample-check requirements are confirmed before manufacturing.

05

Inspection, packing and shipment

Before shipment, packing method and loading details are coordinated. Buyers can request photos, short videos or a factory video audit for order confidence.

OEM Review Checklist

Review pointWhy it mattersWhat buyer should provide
Finished concrete sizeDetermines mould cavity, wall thickness, material choice and packing volume.Length, width, height, diameter, thickness, weight if known.
Demoulding directionUndercuts or reverse angles may require structure changes.Section drawing, side/top photos and installation orientation.
Surface pattern or logoLetters and textures must not block release from the mould.Artwork, marking position, letter height and sample reference.
Material choicePP, ABS and PE suit different rigidity, weight and cost expectations.Use frequency, concrete part size, target budget and local use conditions.
Packing and shipmentLarge or mixed moulds need carton/pallet/loading discussion before final quote.Quantity, mixed SKUs, destination country and requested documents.

Best files to send

  • 2D CAD drawing, PDF drawing or 3D model if available.
  • Finished concrete dimensions and weight if known.
  • Sample photos from front, side, top and bottom.
  • Application: road, drainage, railway, power, PV, slope or landscaping.
  • Quantity, destination country and target delivery timing.
  • Any logo, marking, surface texture, chamfer or local-standard requirement.

Common OEM mould applications

  • Local kerbstone / road curb profiles.
  • Drainage channel, ditch cover and catch basin parts.
  • Inspection well covers, well rings and well bricks.
  • Cable trench, power utility and telecom channel parts.
  • Slope protection, rail transit, PV foundation and landscaping components.
