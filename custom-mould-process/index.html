Custom Mould Process from Drawing to Shipment

A clear OEM workflow for overseas buyers who need plastic moulds for non-standard precast concrete parts, local road profiles or project-specific components.

01

Send drawing, dimensions or sample photos

Share the finished concrete product size, cross-section drawing, application and target quantity. If no drawing is available, send clear photos from multiple angles with a ruler or reference size.

02

Feasibility review

We check whether the shape is suitable for plastic mould production, including demoulding direction, wall thickness, corners, surface patterns and expected concrete use.

03

Material and quotation discussion

ABS, PP and PE options are reviewed according to product size, use frequency, rigidity, weight and target cost. For mixed orders, we can discuss several mould types together.

04

Mould design and production

After confirmation, the mould structure is arranged for production. For complex OEM profiles, details such as logo, letters, chamfer and reinforcement are confirmed before manufacturing.

05

Inspection, packing and shipment

Before shipment, packing method and loading details are coordinated. Buyers can request photos, video checks or a factory video audit for order confidence.

Best files to send

  • 2D CAD drawing, PDF drawing or 3D model if available
  • Finished concrete dimensions and weight if known
  • Sample photos from front, side, top and bottom
  • Application: road, drainage, railway, power, PV, slope or landscaping
  • Quantity, destination country and target delivery timing

Need OEM mould review?

Send your drawing or sample photo. We will check catalogue alternatives or custom mould feasibility before quotation.
